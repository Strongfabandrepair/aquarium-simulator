# User guide

## First tank and daily care

Start with the [README](../README.md), retaining the same browser profile and address. Choose a 20/55/120-gallon scenario or Design a custom tank. Dimensions are centimeters; cube sides are coupled and cylinder width is diameter.

Buy the tank, equipment and substrate/live rock from Store. Prepare water before stocking. Synthetic decorative rocks have no biological filtration credit. Follow water measurements and the journal through salt mixing and fishless cycling. Ghost feeding supports the cycle exercise. Purchases remain gated until completion; zero nitrate after filtration does not by itself prevent a qualified cycle completing.

Pause and 1x/5x/20x affect simulated consequences as well as progress. Observe conditions before leaving accelerated time running. Disable placement editing to orbit the camera.

Read livestock requirements/cautions before purchase; budget/capacity checks can block it. Select a food in Maintenance and Feed Livestock. Cleaner-only tanks can be fed; mixed food does not satisfy the mandarin's modeled copepod requirement. Overfeeding adds waste. Visible food is an offering animation, not a prey ecosystem.

Healthy nourished animals grow toward species limits over simulated days; coral also needs light. Actual models grow, but a few real seconds may show little change. Poor conditions can halt growth or cause death. Remove remains stops subsequent decay load. Clownfish can prefer living placed anemones, but anemones are optional and real hosting is not guaranteed. Puffer predation and coral/anemone stinging are not fully simulated.

## Aquascape and backgrounds

The six repeatable decorations are limestone pebble, purple boulder, slate shelf, sandstone arch, ship and chest. A purchase lacking free floor space is rejected before charging.

1. Open Aquascape editor, enable editing and select a purchased item.
2. Choose Move selected or click an object while editing.
3. Point at an upward-facing surface: green previews are valid; red explains collision, support, boundary or headroom problems.
4. Rotate in 15-degree steps with buttons/arrow keys.
5. Click a valid surface or Save placement; Escape/Cancel discards the preview.

Collapse the panel to expose the tank. Disable editing to orbit. Move dependent items before moving/resizing their supporting rock. The decor slider supports 25-200% of original size, bounded to 2.5-50 cm, without compounding repeated edits. Previewing is not saving. Corals grow biologically instead.

Needs space means a colony outgrew its placement. Use Place / move frag to find room; health continues in the abstract tray.

Blue Ocean Depths, Distant Reef Panorama and Kelp Forest Panorama are exterior backdrops. Click an owned background to switch free or the active one to hide it. They consume no swimming space and provide no filtration.

## Profiles and troubleshooting

Profile switches preserve the outgoing tank. Saves include dimensions, animals, growth/meals, equipment, placements/sizes and backgrounds. With the Node service, acknowledged saves go to SQLite; localStorage retains recovery data. Read status: local-only is not a database acknowledgement. Conflict recovery can preserve backup profiles.

Cookies identify collections. Clearing localStorage can recover SQLite profiles if the identity cookie remains. Clearing cookies, changing browsers or switching localhost/127.0.0.1 can show a different collection. No accounts/cross-device synchronization exist. Read [operations](operations.md) before clearing storage. Reset intentionally deletes; do not use it to recover missing profiles.

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase blocked | Cycle, budget, capacity and species requirements |
| Cycle stalled | Unpaused time, ghost feeding, ammonia/nitrite and journal; nitrate zero alone is not failure |
| Animal stationary | Pause, health and behavior; hovering differs from cruising |
| Coral absent | Needs-space status, size and support |
| Placement blocked | Dependents, collisions, ledge width and boundaries |
| Growth absent | Simulated age, food/light, health and adult cap |
| Profiles missing | Exact browser/address, identity cookie and database path; do not reset |
| Save error | Server/storage permissions; preserve recovery data |
| Slow/no scene | Population/detail, WebGL/hardware acceleration and console errors |

## Learning limits

This is practice for observation, planning, feeding, cycling and maintenance. Growth timing is illustrative, cleanup health is per cohort, colony growth scales meshes and food offerings do not simulate sustainable prey populations. Chemistry volume does not subtract displacement. Full disease, compatibility, stinging, tank engineering and calibrated light/flow are outside the model. Research real animal care, measure actual chemistry and plan acclimation/quarantine. Simulation success is not a real-world safety guarantee.
