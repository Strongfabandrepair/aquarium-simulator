Quick Start
  • Buy your tank, stand, substrate, live rock, salt mix, heater, powerhead, protein skimmer, and test kit.
  • Mix saltwater to 35 ppt, then start the nitrogen cycle.
  • Test ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate daily until the cycle finishes.
  • Use the Ghost Feed option during cycling to supply ammonia without adding fish.
  • Only add fish after ammonia and nitrite read 0 ppm and nitrate is present.
