# Aquarium decor, companions and growth

## Decorating a tank

The equipment store includes six repeatable decorations: a 7 cm ivory limestone pebble, 18 cm purple coralline boulder, 22 cm slate reef shelf, 24 cm sandstone arch, 25 cm sunken sailing ship and 13 cm treasure chest. These are aquarium-safe decorative replicas with distinct geometry, colors and surface detail. They do not contribute biological-filtration credit.

Purchases find an unoccupied substrate location before charging. A tank without enough room rejects the purchase. Use the **Aquascape editor** to select purchased decorations, move them by clicking a valid surface, and rotate in 15-degree steps. Positions persist with the profile. Existing support and overlap rules apply.

Three purchasable backgrounds add visual depth without occupying swimming space: **Blue Ocean Depths**, **Distant Reef Panorama** and **Kelp Forest Panorama**. Click an owned backdrop to switch without paying again; click the active backdrop to hide it. Background ownership and selection persist. These are exterior scenery, not living plants or biological filtration.

## New animals

The catalog contains 37 entries: 15 fish, 13 coral/anemone entries and nine cleanup companions. New choices are the Saddle Valentini Puffer, Orchid Dottyback, Banded Serpent Star and Bubble-tip Anemone.

The puffer has a rounded body, dark saddles and spots, yellow fins and no pelvic fins. The serpent star has five articulated arms and scavenges near the substrate; it consumes meaty food and does not remove algae. Anemones have animated bulb-tipped tentacles and an oral disk. They are animals distinct from true corals and have no modeled skeletal calcium or alkalinity consumption.

Clownfish use a living, placed anemone as a preferred home when available. Dead or unplaced anemones are excluded. This is an illustrative association: a clownfish does not require an anemone, and hosting is not guaranteed in a real aquarium. Anemones need stable mature conditions, appropriate light and flow, guarded pumps and space from other animals. Puffers are not reef-safe companions; consult the catalog cautions before planning a mixed reef. Predation and stinging are not fully simulated.

## Visible maturation

Fish, corals/anemones and cleanup companions change their actual rendered size as their saved physical size grows. Healthy, appropriately nourished animals approach species-specific adult limits; coral growth also requires light. Poor conditions stop growth rather than forcing it. Growth is gradual in simulated days, so it is not expected to be obvious after only a few real-time seconds.

Saved maturity reconstructs missing size using the same curved growth function as normal updates. Invalid oversized saves are clamped to species limits, and rendered size cannot exceed the catalog adult target. Colony growth expands the existing mesh rather than simulating new branches or reproduction. The timing is an educational approximation, not a prediction of a particular animal's growth rate.
